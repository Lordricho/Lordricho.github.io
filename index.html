About Me

Building Secure Digital Solutions for Africa and Beyond.

Richard Ayooluwa Ogunyemi

Computer Software Engineer | Founder & CEO of Softlink Technology

I am a Computer Software Engineer passionate about building secure, scalable and innovative software solutions that solve real-world problems. Through Softlink Technology, I focus on creating digital platforms that empower businesses, educational institutions, financial organizations and government agencies with modern technology.
